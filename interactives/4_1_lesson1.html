User Guide How to Use This Interactive Visual Lab
1. Choose a Concept Mode (Top-Left) Switch between 1. Cartesian & Powers of i, 2. Four Operations (+, −, ×, ÷), 3. Equality & Linear Equations, and 4. Square Roots √(p + qi).
2. Adjust Sliders & Operation Presets Drag the real and imaginary sliders to see how conjugate multiplication realises denominators into c² + d², or how equating real and imaginary parts solves for unknowns.
3. Verify with TI-84 Readout & Exam Check Compare the step-by-step algebraic derivation and complex-plane preview on the right against the simulated TI-84 Plus CE (a+bi mode) output, then complete the 3-question exam check.
