Why You Must Expand Compound Angles First:
• The approximation sin u ≈ u only holds when u ≈ 0.
• In sin(π6 + θ), the argument π6 + θ is near π6 ≈ 0.5236, not near 0!
• Expanding sin(A + B) = sin A cos B + cos A sin B isolates cos θ and sin θ whose arguments are near 0, allowing safe substitution of cos θ ≈ 1 − ½θ² and sin θ ≈ θ.
