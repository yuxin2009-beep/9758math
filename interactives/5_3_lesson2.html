Why Pythagorean Identities Unlock These 5 Forms
1. Even Squares (tan² ax & cot² ax):
• 1 + tan² ax = sec² ax ⇒ tan² ax = sec² ax − 1.
• 1 + cot² ax = cosec² ax ⇒ cot² ax = cosec² ax − 1.
Both sec² ax and cosec² ax are standard MF27 derivatives (1a tan ax and −1a cot ax).
2. Odd Powers (sin3 x, cos3 x, tan3 x):
• Peel off one linear factor to act as f′(x), leaving an even power:
  – sin3 x = sin x(1 − cos² x)
  – cos3 x = cos x(1 − sin² x)
  – tan3 x = tan x(sec² x − 1) = tan x sec² x − tan x

MF27 Factor Formula Reference & Sign Guardrails
MF27 Product-to-Sum Conversions:
• sin A cos B = 12[sin(A + B) + sin(A − B)]
• cos A sin B = 12[sin(A + B) − sin(A − B)]
• cos A cos B = 12[cos(A + B) + cos(A − B)]
• sin A sin B = 12[cos(A − B) − cos(A + B)]
Exam Tip on sin A sin B: In MF27, the formula is listed as cos P − cos Q = −2 sin P+Q2 sin P−Q2. Notice the negative sign: sin A sin B = −½[cos(A + B) − cos(A − B)] = +½[cos(A − B) − cos(A + B)]!
