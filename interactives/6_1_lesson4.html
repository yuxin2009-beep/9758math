Interactive Probability Tree & Sample Space Zoom Lab
1. Conditional Definition
P(A | B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B) (provided P(B) > 0).
Conditioning on B restricts the sample space from 1 down to P(B).
2. Multiplication Law
Multiply along a branch path:
P(A ∩ B) = P(A) × P(B | A).
Note that P(B | A) + P(B′ | A) = 1 at every node!
3. Reverse Conditioning (Total Probability)
To find P(A | B) from a tree:
P(A | B) = [P(A)P(B|A)] / [P(A)P(B|A) + P(A′)P(B|A′)].
9758 Exam Misconception Alert — Confusing P(A | B) with P(B | A):
  • The Prosecutor's / Screening Fallacy: Even if a rapid test is 94% accurate when infected (P(+ | Infected) = 0.94), if the infection is rare (P(Infected) = 0.05), most positive results come from the huge healthy group (0.95 × 0.08 = 0.076 vs. 0.05 × 0.94 = 0.047)!
  • Always check the given condition: The event after the vertical bar | is your new denominator.
