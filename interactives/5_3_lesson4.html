Integration by a Given Substitution (Indefinite Integrals)

Transform ∫ g(x) dx into a pure u- or θ-integral, integrate, and back-substitute into x

5-Step Indefinite Substitution Protocol (9758 H2 Math) Never Mix x and u in the Same Integral & Always Back-Substitute!
Step 1: Differentiate Find dudx (or dxdu) from the given substitution.
Step 2: Replace dx Substitute dx = (dxdu) du into the integral.
Step 3: Eliminate All x Rewrite any leftover x-terms purely in terms of u.
Step 4: Integrate in u Apply power, ln, partial fractions, or trig rules in u.
Step 5: Back-Substitute Replace u back in terms of x and add + C!
