5.5 Lesson 1 • Visualising Differential Equations
What is a Differential Equation? An equation relating an independent variable (x), an unknown function (y), and one or more of its derivatives (such as dydx). Geometrically, it prescribes the tangent gradient at every point (x, y).
What Does It Mean to Solve One? Finding a relationship between y and x (free of derivatives) that satisfies the equation identically. Integrating introduces an arbitrary constant giving a general solution (family of curves); substituting an initial condition (x0, y0) yields one particular solution.
