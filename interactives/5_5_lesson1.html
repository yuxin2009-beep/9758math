User Guide How to Use This Interactive Slope Field & Particle Lab (20 Mins)
1. Switch Between the Two Differential Equations Compare dy/dx = 2x (purely x-dependent, vertical shifts y = x² + C) against dy/dx = 0.5y (purely y-dependent, vertical scalings y = Ae0.5x).
2. Click on the Canvas to Drop Particles Click anywhere on the slope field to place an initial condition (x₀, y₀). Watch particles flow along tangent segments and trace unique particular solution curves.
3. Probe the y = 0 Equilibrium Barrier In dy/dx = 0.5y, drop particles above (y₀ > 0), on (y₀ = 0), and below (y₀ < 0) the x-axis to see why solution curves never cross y = 0 when A changes sign.
