1. Unnumbered vs. Numbered Circle
An unnumbered circle has rotational symmetry ⇒ (n − 1)! ways. Once seats are numbered (or a reference landmark exists), all n rotations become distinct ⇒ n! ways!
2. Grouping ("Together")
Tie the k items into 1 Super-Unit. Arrange all units externally, then multiply by internal orders (k! if distinct, or k! / r₁! if repeated).
3. Slotting ("Separated")
Never use Total − Together when k ≥ 3! Seat the other m items first, then slot the k separated items into the available gaps (ᵐ⁺¹Pₖ in a line, ᵐPₖ in a circle).
