3.1 Lesson 2 • Scalar (Dot) Product & 3D Projections
3D Scalar Product a · b & Angle θ Between Vectors
9758 Misconception Alert (Scalar Product & Projections):
  • Length of projection vs. Projection vector: The length of projection of a onto b is a non-negative scalar |a · b̂| = |a · b| / |b| (always take the modulus if θ is obtuse!). The projection vector of a onto b is a vector (a · b̂)b̂ = ((a · b) / |b|²) b (no modulus, and always divide by |b|², not |b|!).
  • No cancellation law for dot products: If a · b = a · c (with a ≠ 0), you cannot cancel a to conclude b = c; it only means a · (b − c) = 0, so a ⊥ (b − c).
