6.2 Lesson 2 Expectation E(X), Variance Var(X) & Standard Deviation

Visualize E(X) as the mean, compare Var(X) formulas, and evaluate E(g(X)).

1. Choose or Customize Distribution
2. Adjust Outcome Weights / Probabilities
TI-84 Plus CE Verification Steps
  1. Press [STAT] > [1: Edit]; enter outcomes x in L1 and P(X = x) in L2.
  2. Press [STAT] > [CALC] > [1: 1-Var Stats].
  3. Set List: L1 and FreqList: L2, then select Calculate.
  4. Read x̄ = E(X), σx = √Var(X), and Σx² = E(X²) (since ΣFreq = 1).
Mean E(X) = μ 1.0000

Distribution Mean & μ ± σ Spread Sum of Deviations Around Mean: ∑ (x − μ) P(X = x) = 0
Proof by Numbers: Definition vs. Computational Formula
Expectation of a Function E(g(X)) = ∑ g(x) P(X = x)
