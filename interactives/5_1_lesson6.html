User Guide How to Use This Interactive Visual Lab
1. Mode A: Applied Optimisation Drag radius $r$ (or drag directly on the 3D cutaway) to morph a cylinder inside a sphere or a folded conical sector, locking onto the exact maximum where $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}r} = 0$ and $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}^2V}{\mathrm{d}r^2} < 0$.
2. Mode B: Connected Rates of Change Run the play/pause time scrubber on a conical tank or sliding ladder to observe live Chain Rule gauges for $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}$, $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}$, and $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}$.
3. Follow the 9758 Exam Protocol Check every step: (i) express the objective in one variable, (ii) solve $\frac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}r} = 0$, and (iii) verify maximum/minimum nature via $\frac{\mathrm{d}^2V}{\mathrm{d}r^2}$ or a sign table.

3. Connected Rates Substitution
Always differentiate the general relation (e.g., $V = \frac{1}{3}\pi h^3 \tan^2\alpha$) before substituting the instant value of $h$ or $x$.
9758 Exam Misconception Alert:
  • Mandatory Verification Step: Solving $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}r} = 0$ alone never earns full marks in an optimisation question—you must justify maximum/minimum nature using $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}^2V}{\mathrm{d}r^2}$ or a First Derivative Sign Table.
  • Differentiate Before Substituting: In connected rates (e.g., $x^2 + y^2 = L^2$), $x$ and $y$ are functions of time $t$. Never substitute numerical values for $x$ or $y$ before differentiating with respect to $t$.
