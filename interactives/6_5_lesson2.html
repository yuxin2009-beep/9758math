Step 1: Exact Model (No CLT)
Since X ~ N(μ₀, σ²), X̄ ~ N(μ₀, σ²/n) holds exactly for any n. Do not cite CLT!
Step 2: TI-84 [1: Z-Test] Input
Press [STAT] → [TESTS] → 1:Z-Test. Select Stats and enter σ (the single-item population SD, not σ/√n).
Step 3: Compare & Conclude
Compare p-value ≤ α (or z_calc in critical region) and write a full contextual verdict.
9758 H2 Math Exam Misconception & Calculator Traps:
  • CRITICAL σ INPUT TRAP: On the TI-84 Z-Test screen, enter the population standard deviation σ (e.g. 6), NOT the standard error σ/√n = 2 and NOT the variance σ² = 36! The TI-84 divides by √n automatically.
  • Doubling the tail area in a 2-tail test: When computing the p-value manually via normalcdf for H₁ : μ ≠ μ₀, multiply the single-tail area by 2: p-value = 2 × P(Z ≥ |z_calc|).
  • Never write "Accept H₀" or "H₁ is proven": Always state "There is sufficient/insufficient evidence at the α% level of significance to conclude that..."
