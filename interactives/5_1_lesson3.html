9758 Syllabus Notes & Common Pitfalls:
  • Implicit Chain & Product Rules: Every $y$-term requires $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}$ by the chain rule—for example, $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}(x^2 y) = 2xy + x^2\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}$, and $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\!\left[\tan^{-1}\!\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)\right] = \dfrac{1}{1 + (y/x)^2}\cdot\dfrac{x\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} - y}{x^2}$.
  • 9758 Syllabus Exclusion: Second derivatives $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}^2y}{\mathrm{d}x^2}$ of parametrically defined curves are excluded from the 9758 H2 Math syllabus.
