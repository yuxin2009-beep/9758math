9758 Misconception Alert (Composite–Inverse Identities):
  • Different domains for f⁻¹f vs. ff⁻¹: f⁻¹f(x) = x holds only for x ∈ D_f, whereas ff⁻¹(x) = x holds only for x ∈ D_{f⁻¹} = R_f.
  • Reverse the order when inverting a composite: Just like putting on socks (f) then shoes (g), undoing gf requires taking off shoes first (g⁻¹) then socks (f⁻¹): (gf)⁻¹ = f⁻¹g⁻¹.
