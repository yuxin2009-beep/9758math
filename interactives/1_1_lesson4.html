9758 Misconception Alert (Composite–Inverse Identities):
  • Different domains for f−1f vs. ff−1: f−1f(x) = x holds only for x ∈ Df, whereas ff−1(x) = x holds only for x ∈ Df−1 = Rf.
  • Reverse the order when inverting a composite: Just like putting on socks (f) then shoes (g), undoing gf requires taking off shoes first (g−1) then socks (f−1): (gf)−1 = f−1g−1.
