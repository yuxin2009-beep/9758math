User Guide How to Use This Interactive 3D Visual Lab
1. Select a Vector Product Mode
Switch between Cross Product a × b, Properties & Parallelism, and Area Applications at the top-left to explore 3D cross products.
2. Drag or Auto-Rotate the 3D Space
Click and drag on the 3D canvas (or toggle Auto-Rotate 3D) to verify that a × b is perpendicular to both a and b via the right-hand rule.
3. Compare Anti-Commutativity & Areas
Use Mode 2 to visualize b × a = −(a × b), or switch to Mode 3 to compute parallelogram area |a × b| and triangle area ½|a × b|.

9758 Misconception Alert (Vector Product & Area Applications):
  • The result of a × b is a VECTOR, not a scalar: Never write a × b = 0 (the scalar zero) when testing if two vectors are parallel — write a × b = 0 (the zero vector) or |a × b| = 0. Also remember the minus sign on the middle j-component!
  • Triangle Area vs. Parallelogram Area: For a triangle ABC, always take two coterminal edge vectors sharing the same vertex (e.g. AB and AC), and multiply by ½: Area(△ABC) = ½ |AB × AC|.
