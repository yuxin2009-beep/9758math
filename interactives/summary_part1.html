1. Laws of Indices & Logarithms

Laws of IndicesLaws of Logarithms ($a > 0, a \neq 1$)
$a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$$\log_a(xy) = \log_a x + \log_a y$
$a^m \div a^n = a^{m-n}$$\log_a\left(\frac{x}{y}\right) = \log_a x - \log_a y$
$(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$$\log_a(x^r) = r\log_a x$
$a^0 = 1, \quad a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}$$\log_a 1 = 0, \quad \log_a a = 1$
$a^{\frac{m}{n}} = \sqrt[n]{a^m}$Change of Base: $\log_a b = \frac{\log_c b}{\log_c a} = \frac{\ln b}{\ln a}$

2. Trigonometry & Radian Measure

Right-Angled & General AnglesNon-Right Triangle & Radian Measure
$\sin\theta = \frac{\text{Opp}}{\text{Hyp}},\ \cos\theta = \frac{\text{Adj}}{\text{Hyp}},\ \tan\theta = \frac{\text{Opp}}{\text{Adj}}$
Complementary: $\sin(90^\circ-\theta)=\cos\theta$, $\cos(90^\circ-\theta)=\sin\theta$
Supplementary: $\sin(180^\circ-\theta)=\sin\theta$, $\cos(180^\circ-\theta)=-\cos\theta$
Negative: $\sin(-\theta)=-\sin\theta$, $\cos(-\theta)=\cos\theta$, $\tan(-\theta)=-\tan\theta$ 		Sine Rule: $\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}$
Cosine Rule: $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos A$
Area of $\triangle$: $\frac{1}{2}ab\sin C$
Arc Length: $s = r\theta$ ($\theta$ in rad)
Sector Area: $A = \frac{1}{2}r^2\theta$ ($\theta$ in rad)
Pythagorean Identities: $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; \tan^2\theta + 1 = \sec^2\theta \;\Longleftrightarrow\; 1 + \cot^2\theta = \csc^2\theta$
