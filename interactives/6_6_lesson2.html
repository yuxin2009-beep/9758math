Meaning of Regression, Method of Least Squares & Residual Sums

1. What Does "Regression" & "Method of Least Squares" Mean?

  • Meaning of Regression: Finding a mathematical equation (a line of best fit) that models how the average value of a response variable changes with an explanatory variable so we can summarise the trend and make estimates.
  • Residual (Error): For each point (xi, yi), the vertical residual is ey,i = yi − ŷi (observed minus predicted y), and the horizontal residual is ex,i = xi − x̂i.
  • Why Square the Residuals?
    1. Points above the line give positive residuals and points below give negative residuals—simply adding Σei would cancel out to zero even for a terrible line.
    2. Squaring (ei²) makes every term non-negative, penalises large outliers more heavily than small errors, and produces a smooth function with a unique analytical minimum.
  • Method of Least Squares: Chooses the unique line that makes the sum of squared residuals as small as mathematically possible (try the green Adjustable Trial Line to verify no other line beats it!).

Regression Line of y on x:  y = a + bx

  • Minimises: Sum of squared vertical residuals, Σ(yi − ŷi)².
  • Use when: x is controlled, OR estimating y given x (both random).
  • Gradient (b): Change in y per +1 unit of x.
  • y-intercept (a): Value of y when x = 0 (check validity!).

Regression Line of x on y:  x = c + dy

  • Minimises: Sum of squared horizontal residuals, Σ(xi − x̂i)².
  • Use when: y is controlled, OR estimating x given y (both random).
  • Intersection: Both lines always meet at (x̄, ȳ).
  • Coincidence: Identical lines only when |r| = 1.

9758 Exam Alert — Interpreting y-Intercept & Choosing the Right Line:
  • When does the y-intercept (a) NOT make practical sense? (1) When x = 0 is physically impossible or nonsensical in context (e.g., 0°C in tropical Singapore or a person with 0 cm height), (2) when it gives a negative value for a non-negative quantity (e.g., negative sales), or (3) when x = 0 lies far outside the observed data range (invalid extrapolation).
  • Never rearrange y = a + bx to solve for x unless x is controlled! If both x and y are random, use x = c + dy to estimate x from y.
