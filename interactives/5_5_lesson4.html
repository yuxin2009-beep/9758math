How to Formulate & Interpret a Differential Equation in Context
1. Sign of Proportional Rate (k > 0):
• Increases at rate ∝ y: dydt = +ky
• Decreases at rate ∝ y: dydt = −ky
2. Net Rate & Connected Rates:
• Mixing / Balance: dQdt = Rate In − Rate Out
• Geometry Chain Rule: dVdt = dVdh · dhdt
