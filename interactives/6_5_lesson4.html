9758 H2 Math Exam Misconception & Algebraic Traps:
  • CRITICAL invNorm SD TRAP: When using invNorm(area, μ₀, σ/√n) to find the critical boundary in terms of x̄ directly, you MUST enter the standard error σ/√n (NOT σ!).
  • Lowercase x̄ for critical regions: State critical regions as inequalities of observed sample means (e.g. x̄ < 496.7 or x̄ > 503.3), never uppercase X̄.
  • 2-Tail |z_calc| inequality: For a 2-tail test, "H₀ is NOT rejected" means −z_crit ≤ (x̄ − μ₀)/(σ/√n) ≤ +z_crit, which yields a bounded interval a ≤ μ₀ ≤ b (whereas "H₀ is rejected" gives two outer rays μ₀ < a or μ₀ > b).
