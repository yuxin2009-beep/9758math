3.3 Lesson 3 • Foot of Perpendicular & Distance from Point to Plane
3D Foot of Perpendicular N from Point P onto Plane Π
9758 Misconception Alert (Point-to-Plane Distance & Foot of Perpendicular):
  • Direction vector of auxiliary line PN is n, NOT a point on Π: Line PN passes through P and is perpendicular to Π, so it is parallel to the normal vector n: r = p + λn.
  • Divide by |n| (NOT |n|²) for Distance, and ALWAYS take Modulus: Using scalar projection of AP onto n̂ (where A is any point on Π with a · n = d), Distance = |AP · n̂| = |p · n − d| / |n|. Forgetting the modulus when P is on the opposite side of Π gives an invalid negative distance!
