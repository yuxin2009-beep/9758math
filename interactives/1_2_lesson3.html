9758 Misconception Alert (Order & Exam Phrasing):
  • Order matters when combining scaling and translation in the same axis: To reach y = f(2x + 3) from y = f(x):
    • If you translate first: translate by −3 units in the x-direction (x ↦ x + 3), then scale parallel to the x-axis by factor ½ (x ↦ 2x, giving 2x + 3).
    • If you scale first: factor out 2 as f(2(x + 3/2)) — scale parallel to the x-axis by factor ½ first (x ↦ 2x), then translate by −3/2 units in the x-direction (x ↦ x + 3/2, giving 2(x + 3/2) = 2x + 3).
  • Use exact 9758 terminology: Always write "translation of k units in the positive/negative x-direction", "scaling parallel to the x-axis (or y-axis) by a scale factor of k", and "reflection in the x-axis (or y-axis)"—never "shift" or "stretch".
