TI-84 Plus CE Workflow for Transformed Data (x in L1, y in L2)
  1. Define Transformed Lists: Highlight the header L3 (or L4) in STAT → Edit and type the transformation formula (e.g., ln(L2) or L1^2 or 1/L1), then press ENTER.
  2. Check Scatter Plot & |r|: Run LinReg(a+bx) on the transformed lists (e.g., Xlist: L1, Ylist: L3). Confirm the scatter diagram is linear and |r| is closer to 1 than the raw data.
  3. Substitute & Back-Transform: Substitute the given x (or y) into the linearised equation first, then invert the transformation (e.g., y = eY) to express the final answer in original units (3 s.f.).
9758 Exam Alert — Common Linearisation Mistakes:
  • Comparing |r| across transformations: When deciding which of two suggested models fits better, choose the transformation whose |r| is closer to 1 (and whose scatter plot shows no curvature).
  • Forgetting to back-transform: If the linearised line is ln y = 1.20 + 0.35x and x = 4, substituting gives ln y = 2.60—you must compute y = e2.60 = 13.5, not leave 2.60 as the final answer!
