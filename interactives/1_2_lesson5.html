9758 Misconception Alert (Parametric Sketching):
  • Always trim to the parameter interval: Eliminating t gives the full Cartesian equation (e.g., an entire ellipse or hyperbola), but if 0 ≤ t ≤ π, you must sketch only the upper half-ellipse and label both endpoints clearly.
  • Open vs. Closed Endpoint Circles: Use a solid dot (•) when the inequality is ≤ or ≥, and a clear open circle (○) when the inequality is strict (< or >).
  • Orientation Arrows: Draw an arrowhead along the curve showing the direction of increasing t, and verify on your GC under MODE → PAR.
