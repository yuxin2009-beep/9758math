9758 Misconception Alert (Derived Graphs):
  • y = |f(x)| vs. y = f(|x|): |f(x)| reflects parts below the x-axis upward (creating sharp corners at x-intercepts), whereas f(|x|) discards x < 0 and reflects the x ≥ 0 branch across the y-axis.
  • In y = 1/f(x): Roots of f(x) = 0 become vertical asymptotes, local minima become local maxima (y_new = 1/y_old), and as f(x) → ±∞, 1/f(x) → 0.
