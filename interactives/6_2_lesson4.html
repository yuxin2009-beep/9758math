Key 9758 Distinction:
• 2X = doubling ONE trial ⇒ Var(2X) = 2²Var(X) = 4Var(X).
• X₁ + X₂ = sum of TWO independent trials ⇒ Var(X₁ + X₂) = Var(X) + Var(X) = 2Var(X).
• X₁ − X₂ = difference of TWO independent trials ⇒ Var(X₁ − X₂) = Var(X) + (−1)²Var(X) = 2Var(X) (variances ADD!).
