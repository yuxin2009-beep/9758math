9758 H2 Math Exam Misconception & Calculator Traps:
  • CRITICAL σ INPUT TRAP: When population variance is unknown, enter the unbiased sample standard deviation s = √s² into the σ: row on the TI-84 Z-Test screen — NOT the variance s², NOT the standard error s/√n, and NOT the biased SD σx!
  • Always divide by n − 1 for s²: Dividing by n gives a biased variance estimate and costs method/accuracy marks.
  • Always include the word "approximately": When invoking CLT for a non-Normal population, omitting "approximately" in X̄ ~ N(μ₀, s²/n) approximately is penalised in 9758 marking schemes.
