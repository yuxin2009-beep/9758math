Step 1: Define Parameter & H₀, H₁
Define μ in words with units. Always write H₀ : μ = μ₀ and match H₁ to the question's directional claim.
Step 2: GC Critical Value
Press [2ND] → [VARS] → 3:invNorm. Use area α (left-tail), 1 − α (right-tail), or 1 − α/2 (2-tail) with μ = 0, σ = 1.
Step 3: Contextual Verdict
If z_calc falls inside the critical region, reject H₀; otherwise do not reject H₀. Conclude in context!
9758 H2 Math Exam Misconception & Syllabus Guardrail:
  • Never write hypotheses using sample mean x̄: Always use the population parameter μ (e.g. write H₀ : μ = 500, never H₀ : x̄ = 500).
  • Use lowercase z or x̄ for critical regions: State critical regions as inequalities of observed values (e.g. z < −1.64 or x̄ < 496.7), reserving uppercase Z and X̄ for random variables.
  • Never use the term "Type I error" (excluded from 9758): Always interpret α in words as "the probability of wrongly rejecting H₀ when H₀ is actually true."
