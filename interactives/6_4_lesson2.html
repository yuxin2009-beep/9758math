Changi Airport Check-In Baggage Lab

Individual check-in bags have mass X ~ N(μ, σ²). Compare the total mass of n independent bags (T = X₁ + … + Xₙ) against n times a single bag (W = nX)!

Single Bag Mean μ (kg) 23.0 kg
Single Bag Std Dev σ (kg) 3.0 kg
Number of Bags n n = 4 bags
Allowance Limit L (kg) 100 kg
Classic 9758 Exam Trap:
• Sum of n independent bags: Var(X₁ + … + Xₙ) = nσ² (averaging cancels extremes!).
• Scaling 1 bag by n: Var(nX) = n²σ² (a heavy bag gets multiplied n times!).
Sum of n Independent Bags: T = ΣXᵢ ~ N(nμ, nσ²) Scaled Single Bag: W = nX ~ N(nμ, n²σ²)
Random Variable Mean Variance Std Dev P(Mass > 100 kg)
T = X₁ + … + Xₙ (n independent bags) 92.0 kg 36.00 (nσ²) 6.00 kg 0.0912
W = nX (n times 1 single bag) 92.0 kg 144.00 (n²σ²) 12.00 kg 0.2525
Notice both T and W share the exact same mean 92.0 kg, but W = 4X has 4 times the variance (144 vs. 36) and twice the standard deviation (12 kg vs. 6 kg), making extreme overweights far more likely!
