9758 Misconception Alert (3D Ratio Theorem & Collinearity):
  • Cross-weights in the Ratio Theorem: If P divides AB internally in the ratio AP : PB = λ : μ, then p = (μa + λb) / (λ + μ) — μ multiplies a and λ multiplies b! (When λ > μ, P is closer to B, so b carries the larger weight λ.)
  • Proving 3 points A, B, C are collinear in 3D: You must show both: (1) AB = k BC for some scalar k (all 3 ratios of x, y, and z components equal k), AND (2) B is a common point.
