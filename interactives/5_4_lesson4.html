2. Derive from First Principles (Cylinder Sum) Read the 4-step Cylinder Slice Derivation and toggle Show N Cylindrical Discs (adjusting N from 4 to 40) to watch Riemann cylinders converge to π∫ r².
3. Sweep 0° → 360° & Complete Exam Check Animate the 3D revolution sweep, inspect the perpendicular circular cross-section πr² at slice x₀ or y₀, and test yourself on the 3-question 9758 Exam Check.
