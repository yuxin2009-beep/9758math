2. Conics & Rational Graphs

CurveKey Features & Asymptotes
Rational
$y = \frac{ax+b}{cx+d}$ or $\frac{ax^2+bx+c}{dx+e}$		 • Vertical asymptote: denominator $= 0$
• Horizontal/Oblique asymptote: carry out long division
• Label asymptote equations, axial intercepts & turning points
Hyperbola
$\frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} - \frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1$		 • Centre: $(h, k)$
• Asymptotes: set RHS $= 0 \Rightarrow y - k = \pm\frac{b}{a}(x - h)$
• GC: [apps] > [Conics] > [3: Hyperbola]
Parabola $(y-k)^2 = 4p(x-h)$ (horizontal) or $(x-h)^2 = 4p(y-k)$ (vertical); vertex $(h, k)$.
Circle & Ellipse • Circle: $(x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2$, centre $(h,k)$, radius $r$
• Ellipse: $\frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} + \frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1$, horizontal radius $a$, vertical radius $b$ (use ZoomSquare)
