User Guide How to Use This Interactive 3D Visual Lab
1. Switch Between the Two Core Topics
Use the top-left tabs to explore 1. Equation of a Line in Three Forms and 2. Determining if a Point is on the Line.
2. Orbit or Snap the 3D Camera
Click and drag anywhere on the 3D canvas—or toggle Isometric, Top View, and Front View—to inspect how point P sits relative to line L in 3D space.
3. Adjust Vectors & Test Point Membership
Slide anchor a, direction d, and parameter λ in Tab 1, or move test point P in Tab 2 to verify whether λx = λy = λz.

Three Equivalent Forms of a Line L in 3D Space
9758 Misconception Alert (Line Equations & Point Membership):
  • All Three Coordinates Must Yield the Same λ: To prove point P lies on line L, equate OP = a + λd and check that λx = λy = λz. Matching only two coordinates means P shares a 2D shadow with L, not a 3D point!
  • Cartesian Form with a Zero Direction Component: If d = (2, 0, −3) passes through (1, 4, 5), never divide by zero—write (x − 1)/2 = (z − 5)/(−3),  y = 4.
