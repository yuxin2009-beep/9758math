3.2 Lesson 1 • Lines in 3D Space (Equations & Relationships)
Vector Equation of a Line in 3D: r = a + λd
9758 Misconception Alert (Lines in 3D Space):
  • Use DIFFERENT parameters for two lines: When testing if L₁ and L₂ intersect, always write r = a₁ + λd₁ and r = a₂ + μd₂ (using distinct symbols λ and μ). Solving x and y gives unique (λ, μ); you must substitute both into the 3rd equation (z) to check consistency (consistent ⇒ intersecting; inconsistent ⇒ skew!).
  • Cartesian form with a zero direction component: If d = (2, 0, −3) passes through (1, 4, 5), do not divide by zero — write (x − 1)/2 = (z − 5)/(−3),  y = 4. Also remember the acute angle between two lines uses cos θ = |d₁ · d₂| / (|d₁||d₂|).
