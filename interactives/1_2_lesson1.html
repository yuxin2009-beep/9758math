User Guide How to Use This Interactive Visual Lab
1. Choose Linear/Linear or Quadratic/Linear Form Switch between Type 1: y = (ax + b)/(cx + d) (horizontal asymptote) and Type 2: y = (ax² + bx + c)/(dx + e) (oblique asymptote) using the top-left tabs.
2. Adjust Coefficients & Inspect Polynomial Division Move the sliders to shift the vertical asymptote and remainder R, and observe how long division isolates the quotient line y = mx + c plus remainder R/(dx + e).
3. Check Turning Points & Asymptote Approaches Watch how changing the sign of remainder R flips the two branches across the asymptotes and determines whether stationary points exist.

y = 2 + 3/(x − 1) Dashed Red: Vertical Asymptote  |  Dashed Blue: Horizontal/Oblique Asymptote
9758 Misconception Alert (Rational Curve Sketching):
  • Always perform polynomial long division first: Writing y = (x² + 2x − 1)/(x − 1) = x + 3 + 2/(x − 1) immediately reveals the oblique asymptote y = x + 3 (as x → ±∞, 2/(x − 1) → 0).
  • Label every asymptote equation and stationary point: In 9758 exams, sketch marks require explicit equations (x = h, y = mx + c), axial intercepts, and exact coordinates of any local maximum/minimum points.
