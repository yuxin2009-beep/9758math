9758 Misconception Alert:
  • Turning-point inequality direction: When there is a gap between turning points, the range is the outside region (y ≤ ylocal max or y ≥ ylocal min), never the interval between them.
  • Inclusive vs. Exclusive bounds: Turning-point values are attained on the curve (use ≤ / ≥ or square brackets [ ]), whereas horizontal asymptotes and holes are never reached (use ≠ or round brackets ( )).
  • Oblique asymptotes do not guarantee Range = ℝ: Even though y → ±∞ along y = mx + c, an interior gap of excluded y-values still occurs whenever the curve has two turning points.
