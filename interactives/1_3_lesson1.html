9758 Misconception Alert (Algebraic vs. Graphical Inequalities):
  • Never cross-multiply by a variable denominator: Multiplying (x + 1)/(x − 2) ≥ 3 by (x − 2) flips the inequality when x < 2. Always bring all terms to one side and combine over a single denominator.
  • Vertical asymptotes are NEVER included: Even when solving ≥ or ≤, any critical value where the denominator equals zero must remain an open circle (strict inequality).
