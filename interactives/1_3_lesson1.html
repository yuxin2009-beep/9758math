1.3 Lesson 1 • Rational & Quadratic Inequalities
Solve: (2x − 1)/(x − 2) > 1
LHS Curve y = f(x) RHS Line y = g(x) Valid Solution Region (x) Vertical Asymptote (Always Open)
9758 Misconception Alert (Rational Inequalities):
  • Never cross-multiply by an expression containing x: Multiplying (2x − 1)/(x − 2) > 1 by (x − 2) reverses the inequality when x < 2. Always bring all terms to the LHS over a single common denominator, or multiply by (x − 2)² > 0.
  • Vertical asymptotes are NEVER included: Even when solving ≥ or ≤, the value of x that makes the denominator zero is undefined and must always be marked with an open circle (○) and strict inequality (< or >).
