User Guide How to Use This Interactive Visual Lab
1. Select an Argand Concept Mode (Top-Left) Explore 1. Modulus & Quadrant Rules, 2. Vector Addition & Subtraction (|z₁ − z₂|), 3. Modulus & Argument Properties, and 4. Principal Range Wrap-Around.
2. Drag Coordinates Across All Four Quadrants Compute the basic angle α = tan−1|y/x| first, then apply each quadrant's rule (α, π − α, −(π − α), or −α) to keep arg(z) ∈ (−π, π].
3. Verify with TI-84 Commands & Exam Check Compare the geometric angle arc and vector geometry on the right with the simulated TI-84 Plus CE (abs & angle) output, then complete the 3-question exam check.
