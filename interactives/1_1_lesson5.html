9758 Misconception Alert (f(x) = f−1(x) & Self-Inverse Functions):
  • Why solve f(x) = x instead of f(x) = f−1(x)? For an increasing function, y = f(x) and y = f−1(x) intersect strictly on the mirror line y = x, avoiding messy square roots or quartic equations.
  • Watch out for decreasing functions! A decreasing function (such as f(x) = 3 − 23x − 127x³) can also intersect f−1(x) at symmetric pairs (a, b) and (b, a) off the line y = x.
