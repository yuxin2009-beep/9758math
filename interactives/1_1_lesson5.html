9758 Misconception Alert (f(x) = f⁻¹(x) & Self-Inverse Functions):
  • Why solve f(x) = x instead of f(x) = f⁻¹(x)? For an increasing function, y = f(x) and y = f⁻¹(x) intersect strictly on the mirror line y = x, avoiding messy square roots or quartic equations.
  • Watch out for decreasing functions! A decreasing function (such as f(x) = 3 − x³) can also intersect f⁻¹(x) at symmetric pairs (a, b) and (b, a) off the line y = x.
