TI-84 Plus CE: Using the Conics App
  1. Press APPS and select Conics.
  2. Choose Circle, Ellipse, Hyperbola, or Parabola, then pick the matching standard form (e.g., (X−H)²/A² − (Y−K)²/B² = 1).
  3. Enter values for A, B, H, K (note: on TI-84 Conics, the denominator under the positive term is always labelled A², even for vertical hyperbolas/ellipses!).
  4. Press GRAPH to sketch, then ALPHA ENTER (SOLVE) to read off Centre, Vertices, and Foci.
How to Find Hyperbola Asymptotes Algebraically
  1. Write the equation in standard form (completing the square if needed):
    (x − h)²/a² − (y − k)²/b² = 1  or  (y − k)²/b² − (x − h)²/a² = 1.
  2. Replace the 1 on the RHS with 0 (as x, y → ±∞, the constant 1 becomes negligible):
    (x − h)²/a² − (y − k)²/b² = 0.
  3. Take square roots on both sides to get both linear asymptotes:
    y − k = ±(b/a)(x − h)  (note: always b/a, where b is under y and a is under x!).
9758 Misconception Alert:
  • TI-84 Conics App vs. 9758 Notation: The TI-84 Conics app always puts A² under the positive term (so for (y−k)²/b² − (x−h)²/a² = 1, the calculator calls b "A" and a "B"). Always keep a paired with x and b paired with y in your written working so your asymptote gradient stays ±b/a.
  • Identifying hyperbola orientation: Look at which squared term is positive, not which denominator is larger.
