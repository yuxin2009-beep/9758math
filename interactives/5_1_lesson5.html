9758 Exam Presentation & Scope Rules:
  • When $f''(c) = 0$: Never conclude "stationary point of inflexion" solely from $f''(c) = 0$ (for example, $y = x^4$ has $f''(0) = 0$ but is a local minimum!). You must fall back on the First Derivative Sign Table.
  • 9758 Syllabus Exclusion: Non-stationary points of inflexion (where $f''(c) = 0$ with sign change in $f''$, but $f'(c) \neq 0$) are not required in the 9758 H2 Math syllabus.
