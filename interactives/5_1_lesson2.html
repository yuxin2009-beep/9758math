9758 Exam Sketching Rules for $y = f'(x)$ from Complex Curves $y = f(x)$:
  • Stationary points → $x$-intercepts: Local maxima/minima become crossing $x$-intercepts of $y = f'(x)$, whereas stationary points of inflexion become touching (bouncing) $x$-intercepts of $y = f'(x)$.
  • Asymptote transformations: A vertical asymptote $x = c$ stays a vertical asymptote $x = c$ on $y = f'(x)$. A horizontal asymptote $y = k$ flattens to $y = 0$ on $y = f'(x)$, while an oblique asymptote $y = mx + c$ becomes a horizontal asymptote $y = m$ on $y = f'(x)$.
