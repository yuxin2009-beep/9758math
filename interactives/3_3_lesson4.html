3.3 Lesson 4 • Two Planes: Intersection, Angle & Parallel Distance
Relationship Between Two Planes Π₁ & Π₂ and Line of Intersection
9758 Exam Strategy & Misconception Alert (Two Planes):
  • Recommended Method for Line of Intersection (No Unknowns): When both planes have numerical coefficients, solve their Cartesian equations simultaneously using the GC (2 equations, 3 unknowns) and let the free variable (usually z) equal λ. It gives both the position and direction vectors in one step without risking sign errors in n₁ × n₂. Reserve d = n₁ × n₂ for questions containing symbolic unknowns or when only the direction vector is needed.
  • Acute Angle & Equalized Normals: Always use modulus cos θ = |n₁ · n₂| / (|n₁||n₂|) for the acute angle between planes, and always scale one equation first so both parallel planes share the exact same normal n before applying |d₂ − d₁| / |n|.
