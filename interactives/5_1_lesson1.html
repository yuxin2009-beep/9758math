9758 Exam Misconception Alert:
  • Always use Radian Mode: The standard derivatives $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}(\sin x) = \cos x$ and $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}(\sin^{-1} x) = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}}$ hold only when angles are measured in radians.
  • Don't forget the Chain Rule multiplier $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}u}{\mathrm{d}x}$: For $y = \sin^{-1}(2x)$, $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{1 - 4x^2}}$; for $y = 5^{3x}$, $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = 3(\ln 5)\cdot 5^{3x}$.
