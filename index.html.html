    9758 Scheme of Assessment

    There are two 3-hour papers, each carrying 50% of the total mark. Use of an approved graphing calculator (GC) without computer algebra system (CAS) is expected for both papers.

    Paper Duration & Weighting Structure & Content Coverage
    Paper 1 3 hours (100 marks, 50%) 10 to 12 questions of varying lengths and marks based on Pure Mathematics (Section A). There will be at least two questions on application of Mathematics in real-world contexts, including those from sciences and engineering, with at least one carrying 12 marks or more.
    Paper 2 3 hours (100 marks, 50%) Section A (40 marks): 4 to 5 questions on Pure Mathematics.
    Section B (60 marks): 6 to 8 questions on Probability and Statistics, including at least one question on application of Mathematics in real-world contexts carrying 12 marks or more.

    Assessment Objectives (AO)

    • AO1 (Understand and apply mathematical concepts and skills in a variety of contexts): Recall and use facts, terminology, notation, and standard mathematical procedures.
    • AO2 (Organise and analyse data and information; formulate and solve problems): Interpret information, formulate problems into mathematical terms, and select appropriate strategies.
    • AO3 (Interpret mathematical results and make inferences; write mathematical explanations and arguments): Justify mathematical statements, prove results, and communicate clear arguments.

    Assumed Knowledge (O-Level Additional Mathematics)

    Knowledge of O-Level Additional Mathematics is assumed: Quadratic functions, equations and inequalities, surds, polynomials and partial fractions, binomial expansions, exponential and logarithmic functions, trigonometric functions, identities and equations, coordinate geometry in two dimensions, and proofs in plane geometry.
